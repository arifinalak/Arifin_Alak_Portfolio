About

I am passionate about Artificial Intelligence and Web Development. I am a Software Developer passionate about building smart, clean, and useful digital solutions. Currently studying at BRAC University, I focus on combining strong back-end logic with creative design thinking.

I mainly work with Python & Flask, building fast and structured APIs. When performance matters, I switch to C / C++ for speed and efficiency. On the front end, I use HTML & CSS to create clean, user-friendly interfaces. I also work with MySQL & MariaDB, making sure data is organized, optimized, and easy to manage.

Beyond coding, I specialize in AI prompt design—turning complex questions into clear, meaningful results. Graphic design is my creative space, helping me build apps that are not only functional but also visually engaging.
